Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Last updated: September 30, 2026

Totup is an expense notebook you tap or talk to. It’s made by InstaPodz, Inc. (“we”). This page explains what happens to what you tell it. The short answer: it stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Totup doesn’t collect your data. We never receive your expenses, your notes or anything else you log.
  • There’s no account, no sign-up and no Totup server.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • Totup Plus, the subscription, is sold and billed by Apple. We never see your card or payment details.
  • What you log is stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • The kid’s notes are written on your iPhone and never leave it.

What Totup keeps, and where

When you log an expense, Totup saves:

  • the amount;
  • what it was, and where, if you said;
  • its category;
  • the date and time;
  • a short note, the kid’s or your own.

It also keeps your budget, any income you add, the count of your free days of logging, and a few settings, such as how far you are in the tour.

All of it is stored on your iPhone, in storage that only the Totup app, its widgets and its Live Activities can read (an App Group container). It isn’t sent to us or to anyone else. Totup has no server to send it to.

Typing, Siri and Shortcuts

You log and ask in the app itself, or through Siri, Type to Siri, Spotlight, the Shortcuts app, the Action Button or a Control.

  • In the app: the line you type after tapping Add goes straight to Totup, on your iPhone. Siri isn’t involved.
  • Siri is Apple’s. Siri handles your request under Apple’s privacy policy and your Siri settings. See Siri, Dictation & Privacy and the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • What Totup receives from Siri is the text of the request, such as “latte at Blue Bottle, six”. It uses that text on your iPhone to save the expense or answer your question.
  • Understanding the sentence (the amount, the category, the item) happens on your iPhone, whether you typed it in the app or said it. Totup’s own rules go first. On iPhones that support it, Apple’s on-device language model helps.

The kid’s notes

Under each expense, a kid leaves a short note. The kid is a made-up character, not a real person.

  • Each note is written on your iPhone by Apple’s Foundation Models framework, the on-device model that is part of Apple Intelligence, or picked from lines built into the app. See Apple Intelligence & Privacy.
  • The model gets the words for the item, its category and the place. It never gets the amount.
  • Notes never leave your iPhone. A note you write yourself stays there too, and the kid never writes over it.
  • Some expenses get no note at all: health, insurance, taxes, debt, donations, childcare, alcohol, tobacco and betting.

Totup Plus and payments

Your first 7 days of logging are free. After that, adding needs Totup Plus, a subscription sold through the App Store.

  • Apple handles the payment. You buy, renew, cancel and get refunds through your Apple Account, under Apple’s terms and the Apple Privacy Policy. We never see your card, your billing details or your Apple Account.
  • What Totup checks: on your iPhone, Totup asks the App Store whether Totup Plus is active, and for which plan and period. The answer stays on your iPhone. Totup doesn’t send it to us.
  • What Apple tells us: Apple gives us sales reports, such as how many subscriptions were sold in each country. They don’t include your name, email address or payment details.
  • Your free days are counted on your iPhone, from the days you logged something. The count isn’t sent to us.
  • Cancelling: in Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting Totup doesn’t cancel it.
  • Refunds are Apple’s: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Without Totup Plus, you can still read, search, share, export and delete everything you logged.

What Totup never does

  • No account, no login, no email address, no phone number.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. Totup doesn’t use the advertising identifier, and it doesn’t track you across apps or websites.
  • No third-party code that collects data: no ad, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No bank connection and no receipt scanning. Totup never reads your photos, contacts or location. If you save your month card to Photos from the share sheet, iOS asks your permission first.
  • We don’t sell or share personal information, because we don’t have any.

What shows on your screen

  • Widgets, the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island show your totals, your latest expense and its note. Anyone who can see your screen can see them. You can remove a widget, or turn off Live Activities in Settings › Totup, at any time.
  • Spotlight can find the things you logged, on your iPhone. Notes are never indexed.
  • Sharing: when you share your month card, iOS opens its share sheet and you choose where it goes. The card shows no amounts. Totup never posts anything by itself.
  • Export: Export my rows makes a CSV file of your rows, amounts and notes included, and opens the same share sheet. You choose where it goes; Totup sends it nowhere by itself.
  • Help’s links open this page and the support page in your browser. Opening them sends nothing from Totup.

Backups and Apple’s diagnostics

  • Backups: if you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, your Totup data can be part of that backup, under your backup settings and Apple’s terms. The backup is yours. Totup doesn’t upload it.
  • Diagnostics: you may turn on Share With App Developers (in Settings, under Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements). Then Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us. They describe how the app runs, not what you logged, and Apple’s privacy policy covers them.

Deleting your data

  • One expense: tap it, then Delete. Or say “Totup undo” to remove the last thing you logged.
  • Everything: delete the app, in Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Totup › Delete App. That removes your expenses, notes, budget and settings from your iPhone.
  • Totup Plus isn’t cancelled by deleting the app. To stop it, cancel it in Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Backups: copies in backups you made stay until those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • On our side there’s nothing to delete, because we never received anything.

Children

Totup is for adults. It isn’t directed to children, and it collects no data from anyone, children included. The kid in the notes is a made-up voice, not a child using the app.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none: Apple handles purchases, and its sales reports to us don’t include your name, email address or payment details. So there’s nothing for us to access, correct, delete or sell. Your data stays on your device, under your control. If you have a question, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If Totup ever starts collecting data, we’ll say so here and in the App Store privacy details before it happens.
